Never rate a market segment as open, thin, uncontested or "white space" on the basis that the vendor pages I happened to read did not mention it. Before the claim goes on a slide or in a brief, run a deliberate search for counter-examples (vendors selling exactly that offer to exactly that segment) and read their pricing or tier pages.

**Why:** On 2026-11-09 (Mbriyo, Nadifor positioning grid) I rated "focused AI upskilling programs for 500 to 5,000 staff firms" as the thin cell, made it the headline of slide 1 and the premise of slide 2. Vas asked "where is the validation that it's thin?" There was none. One follow-up search found Section (tiers for sub-1,000-person companies), Leland (before and after assessment, any size) and OneRange (100+ employees) selling there. Three slide versions and one grid had to be corrected. The same error appeared on 2026-10-28 ("only uncontested cell"). Vas did not state a rule; the rule is my interpretation of his question.

**How to apply:** Any competitive map, positioning grid or VC evaluation. Treat "nobody does X for Y" as a hypothesis, state how many vendors were checked, search for disconfirming vendors first, and if the claim survives, label it as supply-side only (not demand).
