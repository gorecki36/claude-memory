Slide headlines in a chapter should read in sequence as one narrative: same subject and voice (e.g. every one a full sentence starting "People..."), parallel phrasing, no telegraphic fragments mixed with sentences. Vas also prefers headlines without percentages; the numbers sit on the chart.

**Why:** On the Citizen Trust readout (2026-10-19) Vas rejected "Comfortable going online with agencies, not with AI deciding for them" after "People with no recent dealings expect..." because the two were not phrased alike, and repeatedly asked to strip numbers out of headlines.

**How to apply:** When editing one headline, read the chapter's headlines in order and align them. Keep a data check behind every word claim ("very few", "most", "easiest") once the number leaves the headline. Related: [[feedback_plain_headlines]], [[feedback_headline_formatting]].
