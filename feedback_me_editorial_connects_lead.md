In a Marketing Embeddings issue (or any lead-plus-items piece), the opening editorial must make a clear, substantive connection to the main story it precedes. End on, or pivot into, the exact theme the lead opens with, so the reader flows straight from the editorial into the lead. Do not write a free-floating intro that ignores the lead.

**Why:** Vas flagged an editorial that read as disconnected from the main story and asked for a clearer link, then said to make it a standing rule. Also: "change tab from Welcome embedders to the first sentence" (put "Welcome back, embedders." on its own line/paragraph, then start the content).

**How to apply:** The intro's spine is the main story. Supporting news points may appear as context, but never as a manufactured through-line ([[feedback_dont_force_connections]], voice-rules rule 9). Codified as rule 10 in `voice-rules-prompt.md`. Related: [[feedback_story_first]].
