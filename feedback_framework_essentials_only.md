When asked for a framework, protocol outline or "starting point", deliver the essentials only: a short list (about 9 or fewer) where every item traces to a source read this session or to our own data, each with a plain status of whether it already exists. Do not pad with items I proposed myself and cannot check.

**Why:** On 2026-11-09 (BHI measurement protocol, Mbriyo) I delivered 32 parameters, 10 per-treatment settings and a 23-row table. Vas: "you made it super complicated. there is no way that most of this can be checked or that you validated the logic." He wanted "something that provides a framework", "simple", with "key variables that we can absolutely confirm", plus an assessment of whether each part is already included, and to be told if that assessment is not possible.

**How to apply:** Before building, cut to what can be confirmed. Show status per item (in place, partly, not in place) with the evidence. State plainly what could not be assessed. List what was left out on purpose in one short box instead of including it. (My interpretation: this applies to any first-pass framework for any role, not only BHI.)
