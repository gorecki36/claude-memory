Keep chat responses short. No multi-section reports, no tables of every finding, no "sources" appendix unless asked. Target 15-20 lines. Lead with the verdict or the single most important finding, then at most 3-5 bullets. Offer depth as a follow-up ("want the rest?") instead of shipping it unprompted.

**Why:** Vas reads in a terminal and has said this many times. A long answer is unreadable there, so an exhaustive review that is technically complete still fails to deliver. Scored a review of two Moloco briefs as "shitty" purely on length.

**How to apply:** Before sending, cut to the decisions Vas has to make. Drop restated source material, drop anything he already knows, drop hedging. If the analysis genuinely needs length, write it to an HTML file in the project folder (see [[feedback_output_format_html]]) and give him a 5-line summary plus the path. Applies to review/analysis answers, not to deliverable files themselves. Related: [[feedback_dont_stop_midtask]], [[feedback_specify_file_paths]].
