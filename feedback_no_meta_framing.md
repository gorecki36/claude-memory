Never use sentences that announce the rhetorical hierarchy of your own argument. Banned constructions include but are not limited to:

- "The sharper objection is [X]."
- "The deeper issue is [X]."
- "The bigger / real / more important point is [X]."
- "What's really going on here is [X]."
- "The harder question is [X]."
- "The interesting thing is [X]."

**Why:** These are AI-writing tells. They scaffold the argument with self-narration instead of just making the argument. Vas has flagged this pattern repeatedly (memory exists because it has come up many times). Reads as performative and condescending. Strip the scaffolding, lead with the substance.

**How to apply:** When reviewing your own draft before sending, search for sentences of the form "The [comparative-adjective] [noun] is [noun]" or "What's really / actually [verb]ing is" and rewrite them so the point lands directly without the meta-frame. Apply across all written output (chat, briefs, vault notes, slides). Stricter than the general "no AI-writing patterns" rule in [[feedback-quality-standards]].
