For Marketing Embeddings, never refer to the publication's content as an "essay." Vas publishes **issues**; the top analytical piece is **the lead** (or "this week's lead" / "this week's read"). Strip the word "essay" from drafts, editorials, signal items, and any cross-references inside the issue.

**Why:** Vas thinks of ME in terms of weekly issues, not standalone essays; "essay" reads as the wrong frame for the format. Called out on the 2026-06-22 (Cannes) issue.

**How to apply:** When a signal item or the editorial references the top piece, write "this week's lead" / "the lead," not "the lead essay." When discussing the deliverable, say "the issue." See also [[feedback-plain-headlines]] (the "quiet" word ban), [[feedback-no-em-dashes]].
