Every left-to-right scale in a deck puts low, negative or "avoid/oppose" on the left in red, and high, positive or "embrace/support" on the right in navy (or the house positive colour). This covers stacked strips, axes, appendix table column order, and side-by-side opinion-group panels.

**Why:** Vas asked for one consistent direction across the Citizen Trust readout (2026-10-19) after slides 9 and 12 ran in opposite directions and slide 15 and several tables put "positive" on the left.

**How to apply:** Audit the whole deck, not just the slide being edited, whenever a scale is added or changed. Related: [[feedback_highlight_number_colors_bars]], [[feedback_deck_design_system]].
