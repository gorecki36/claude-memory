Never use "the part nobody's pricing" or "the part nobody is doing" or any variant of "and that's the part nobody is X." This is a classic AI writing pattern.

**Why:** Vas flagged it as "classic AI" and said "I don't want to see it ever again." It's a false-insight construction that sounds profound but says nothing.

**How to apply:** If you find yourself writing "and that's the part nobody is [verb]ing," delete the sentence and make the actual point directly. Say what the thing IS, not that nobody is noticing it.
