Never use reveal-scaffolding phrases that tee up a point instead of making it: "is the part almost nobody slowed down to check," "what nobody noticed," "here's the thing," "the part everyone missed," "few people realized." Just say what the thing is or means.

**Why:** It's rhetorical filler and reads as AI-speak. The setup adds words and delays the actual claim. Caught in the ME "Chart Everyone Got Wrong" editorial: "What it tracks... is the part almost nobody slowed down to check" → "But it does not measure what the headlines assumed... More on that below."

**How to apply:** When tempted to write "X is the thing nobody noticed," delete the frame and state X. Same family as [[feedback_no_meta_framing]] ("the sharper objection is...", "what's really going on is..."). Part of the universal anti-AI-writing baseline in [[feedback_quality_standards]].
