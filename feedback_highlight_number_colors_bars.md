When a slide highlights a number next to a chart (e.g. "68% use AI at least weekly" beside the Q6 frequency bars), the bars that sum to that number stay the strong colour (black in the house template) and every other bar goes gray.

**Why:** Vas asked for it on the Citizen Trust Tracker deck (2026-09-24): without it the reader cannot see which bars the hero number is built from.

**How to apply:** Any hero/stat card that is a sum of categories in the adjacent chart. Mean-score heroes (no subset of bars) are exempt. Related: [[feedback-deck-design-system]], [[feedback-slides-fill-page]].
