Only add a side-insight panel (america.gov-style serif statement + 1-2 sentences + link to an appendix data slide) when the subgroup cut shows a real, significant difference. If the read is "the order holds in almost every group", skip the panel and its appendix slide entirely; don't fill the space with exceptions.

**Why:** Vas cut the slide 3/4 panels on the Citizen Trust readout (2026-10-09) as "no finding, no need to add the details", and kept only slide 8 (paying splits by age and location). He also cut a sentence restating the panel heading as redundant.

**How to apply:** Run the subgroup cuts first; put a panel only where a difference changes the reading. Keep panel body to the new information, never a restatement of the heading. Related: [[feedback_short_responses]], [[feedback_never_invent_unflagged]].
