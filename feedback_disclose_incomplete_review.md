Never recommend an option without disclosing whether the full set of alternatives was reviewed. If you only examined 2 of 13 categories, say so before making the recommendation, not after the user asks.

**Why:** Vas caught me recommending the ESOMAR Breakthrough Research Methodology category when I had only found details on 2 of 13 categories. He said: "You should be more transparent. I could have taken your recommendation if I didn't ask, and I wouldn't have realized that you hadn't reviewed the list."

**How to apply:** Before any recommendation where options exist (award categories, vendors, approaches, tools), explicitly state: "I reviewed X of Y options. Here's what I found." If you couldn't access the full list, say that first. The recommendation comes after the disclosure, not before.
