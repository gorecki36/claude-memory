Before putting a figure in an award entry or deck as "MMA's" result, open the note and check what the work actually is and where the number originated.

**Why:** 2026-10-09, Greg's Digiday AI Leader entry. I listed "60,000+ ad tests with AT&T" and the 52.5% finding as an MMA AI study. The vault note (mma/AT&T — Brand Creative Integrity) is an ad-hoc evidence review for AT&T citing third-party papers (Danaher 2008 etc.), not MMA research and not about AI. Vas: "where did you find this shit". Same session, "300 campaigns / +24%" for AURA traced only to the role charter (mma-global/CLAUDE.md), with no primary source; Vas did not recognise it.

**How to apply:** For every claim in external copy, trace it to a primary MMA deliverable (deck, report, results file). A charter line or a subagent summary is a pointer, not a source. If the note is a literature review, it cannot be presented as MMA's own study. Related: [[feedback_verify_claims]], [[feedback_never_invent_unflagged]].
