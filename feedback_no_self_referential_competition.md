In Marketing Embeddings (and Vas's writing generally), never write the publication as if it is talking to itself or scoring points. Banned moves:
- Self-reference to our own argument as an authority: "this week's lead says…", "as the lead argues…", "as we noted above…".
- Competition / concession framing: reality, a festival, a company "conceding," "vindicating," "proving us right," "the trouble is downstream" as a scoreboard line.
- Cheap-journalism flourish that treats the news as a contest between our thesis and the world.

**Why:** Vas: "this is us talking to ourselves… this is not a competition. we are not doing cheap journalism." The newsletter reports on the world and connects items plainly; it does not narrate its own cleverness or keep score. Caught on the 2026-06-22 Cannes issue editorial.

**How to apply:** Make each point directly about the world. State the finding, then state the connection as a fact about reality, not as confirmation of our argument. Drop "the lead says / we said." Let the reader draw the line; supply the facts that draw it.
