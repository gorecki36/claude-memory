Slides and charts must use the ENTIRE frame. Vas has repeatedly flagged charts that leave most of the page empty, use small fonts, and look unfinished/ugly. This is a recurring 1/5 issue.

**Why:** decks present full-screen; empty space + small type reads as amateur and wastes the canvas.

**How to apply (every slide, before showing):**
- **Large type:** hero numbers 50px+, item labels 16px+, slide headlines 30px+. No tiny fonts.
- **Fill the frame, not busy, not empty:** size or vertically center the content block so it spans the available area. Never leave a big empty bottom half. If a chart only needs the top, make it taller (full-height cards, bigger bars/numbers) or center it.
- **Cards + icons where relevant:** group items into cards; add a relevant icon per card/stage; **center the content within each card** (icon -> label -> number, centered).
- **Self-check:** after building, screenshot the slide and look at it. If there's a large empty region or the fonts look small, redesign (bigger, centered, carded) BEFORE showing Vas. Do not ship a half-empty slide.

Worked example: the iOpex value-chain slides were rebuilt from a top-loaded bar list into 5 full-height cards (icon + stage + 58px % numbers, centered) that fill the frame.
