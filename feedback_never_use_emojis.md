Never, ever use emojis. Not as icons in HTML/diagrams, not in slides, not in prose, not in chat responses. Stated with maximum emphasis ("never, ever, ever, ever").

**Why:** Emojis read as amateurish and off-brand for Vas's deliverables (Moveo, MMA, ME, decks, vasteams.com). They cheapen otherwise polished work.

**How to apply:** For icons in HTML/diagrams, use inline SVG line icons (stroke-based, currentColor) like the source Moveo diagram did, or clean typographic/geometric marks. Never Unicode emoji glyphs (💬🧠⚙🛡🗄 etc.). Geometric shapes (arrows via SVG) are fine; pictographic emoji are not. Pairs with [[feedback_no_chip_pills]] and [[feedback_no_serif]] as house-style hard rules.
