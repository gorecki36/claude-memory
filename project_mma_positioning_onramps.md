MMA Direction & Positioning work started at the 12 Oct 2026 meeting (Greg, Peter Cowden, Toby/Lafayette American, Natalie, Vas). Vas maps five ways in for CMOs: FOMO, spotlight, confidence, knowledge, boldness. Evidence base: the 2023 sales-call objection analysis (~/Documents/mma-global/strategy/RePositioning/MMA Sales Call Objections 2023 - Analysis 1-00.html).

"Confidence" means the CMO's confidence and standing INSIDE their own organization (with the CEO, CFO and board, proving marketing drives growth). It does not mean confidence about buying a membership. Vas corrected this on 2026-10-14 after I mapped confidence to "I can't buy it alone."

**Why:** The positioning hypothesis rests on MMA's CMO Agenda data (64% of major CMOs want more confidence in marketing's ability to drive growth). Treating it as purchase confidence confuses the buying barrier with the CMO's underlying need.

**How to apply:** When mapping objections or messaging to the confidence way in, look for evidence of the CMO's standing in the org (proving value to the CFO/CEO, budget pressure, not owning the levers). Keep purchase friction (budget cycle, sign-off, authority) as a separate buying-process issue. Related: [[feedback_never_invent_unflagged]].
