Never describe a study by its sample size in headlines, titles, cover lines or body copy ("Four themes from 103 senior marketers", "103 senior marketers, fielded May to July"). The n lives in the methodology slide and the per-chart base footnote only.

**Why:** Vas, 2026-10-23, on the Knotch survey report exec summary: "replace this with something more insightful, and never, ever talk about 103 marketers again." A headline built on the n says nothing; it should carry the finding.

**How to apply:** Exec summary and chapter headlines state the insight (e.g. "The customer journey has changed faster than marketers can measure or fund it"). Keep "Base: n of N answered" in footnotes and the n on the methodology slide. Related: [[feedback_plain_headlines]], [[feedback_headlines_read_as_one_story]].
